Before you buy

What size fits a small kitchen?

Measure the drawer or counter spot first, then choose the compact version — most gadgets here come in a small-space size.

What should I check before buying?

Look for dishwasher-safe parts and a storage footprint you can live with every day, not just on cooking days.

One gadget or a set?

Buy the single tool you reach for weekly first. Sets only pay off when you will use at least half the pieces.
