Before you buy

What fits under a standard bed?

Most frames give you 6–7 inches of clearance — low-profile containers slide in without lifting the bed.

Are over-door organizers safe for doors?

Choose padded hooks and check the gap between door and frame so it still closes cleanly.

Bags or hard bins?

Vacuum bags for soft things like bedding; hard bins for anything you access every week.
