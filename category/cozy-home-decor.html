Before you buy

How do I make a room feel cozy fast?

Layer three things: warm light low in the room, one soft textile, one natural texture like wood or ceramic.

Which accent color should I choose?

Pick one deep color — emerald, burgundy or navy — and repeat it three times in the room.

Seasonal decor in a small home?

Swap textiles and table layers instead of adding objects; the room changes without new clutter.
